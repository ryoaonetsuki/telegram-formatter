🔗 Links

Link tools

Share links, bot deep links, cleaning, extraction, QR codes and UTM tags — all validated in your browser. (For basic profile/channel/post links, see the Link Builder.)

📤 Share Link Generator

Build a t.me/share/url link that opens Telegram's share sheet with your link and text.

—

🤖 Deep Link Builder

Build bot deep links: t.me/yourbot?start=payload (also startgroup for adding to groups). Payloads are validated to Telegram's allowed characters.

—

🧹 Link Cleaner

Strip tracking parameters (utm_*, fbclid, gclid, etc.) from a URL, keeping the real address.

—

🔍 Link Extractor

Pull every unique URL out of pasted text.

—

👤 Mention Extractor

Pull every unique @username out of pasted text.

—

🔳 QR Code Generator

Make a QR code for any t.me link (or any text) and download it as a PNG. Generated locally with the qrcode-generator library (MIT) — nothing is uploaded.

QR library: qrcode-generator by Kazuhiko Arase, MIT license, vendored locally in assets/qrcode.js.

🏷️ UTM Builder

Add utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign to a URL for tracking where traffic came from.
